## 5. Vendor Listing Process — Step by Step

Synthesised from Capterra vendors page, GetApp Profile Guidelines (May 4 2026), G2 Digital Markets Engaged Buyers guide, and Software Advice vendor page.

### 5.1 Eligibility (GetApp Profile Guidelines — incorporated into General Vendor Terms)

- **[OBSERVED FACT]** Must be **B2B or B2C** — GDM does **not** list marketplaces/database websites, personal productivity solutions, product clones, or personal apps.
- **[OBSERVED FACT]** Must be a **packaged (pre-built/off-the-shelf) solution** — no custom/bespoke software.
- **[OBSERVED FACT]** Must **fit, at GDM's sole discretion, within an existing category.**
- **[OBSERVED FACT]** Must be **publicly available with a call to action** highlighting an offer on sale to public: trial, demo, request info. GDM will **not list** products whose only CTA is "join a waitlist."
- **[OBSERVED FACT]** Products in **beta** may be listed when there is a working website promoting the product, an offer on sale to public, and **at least one review within the first calendar year**.
- **[OBSERVED FACT]** Must be listed under **product/service name on vendor's owned website** — no reseller/integrator/consultant names, unless vendor expressly authorizes reseller. When parent company requests lead gen, parent takes precedence and reseller is disabled.
- **[INFERENCE]** These rules gatekeep small/indie tools that are still waitlisted, bespoke agencies re-badged as SaaS, and clone products — keeping GDM catalog defensible for SEO.

### 5.2 Creation paths

- **[OBSERVED FACT]** GDM will **either create product listings itself from publicly available information** or **work with vendor** to create/update a listing reflecting product's features. Vendor-provided info informs certain elements, but **final copy subject to GDM content team discretion**. Additional sources: public info + content derived from reviews. **[OBSERVED FACT]** GetApp Guidelines.
- **[OBSERVED FACT]** Vendor workflow for PPC: 1) Complete profile + collect reviews → 2) Boost visibility with PPC campaigns → 3) Convert traffic into leads. "Log into your account to submit your PPC campaign request or connect with your account representative" — vendor submits request → rep approves → spend starts. **[OBSERVED FACT]** Engaged Buyers guide + program page.
- **[OBSERVED FACT]** Free estimate CTA: "Get a FREE Estimate — Bids start at $2/click. Contact us for personalized estimate, including traffic stats." Form fields include required fields (name/email/product/category implied). **[OBSERVED FACT]** Capterra vendors page.

### 5.3 Content & asset guidelines

- **[OBSERVED FACT]** Description must be **unique (not copied)**, true product overview, third-person ("Do not write in first person e.g. we, our, us"), no superlatives ("best, most, fastest"), no offensive/defamatory language, no CTAs or PII (phone/email/fax/URL) in description, no corporate suffixes (Inc, LLC), no repeated punctuation/excessive caps, no line breaks/symbols/HTML. Product name must match vendor website. Destination URLs must work. **[OBSERVED FACT]** GetApp Guidelines — The Do's/Don'ts.
- **[OBSERVED FACT]** Screenshots: at least one strongly encouraged; must display **real UI**, no stock images or marketing jargon. Video: English only, about the product, YouTube/Vimeo/Wistia link. Submitting grants GDM right to use asset in ordinary course. **[OBSERVED FACT]** GetApp Guidelines.
- **[INFERENCE]** GDM content team rewrites vendor fluff into neutral marketplace voice — which explains why all three sites' profiles sound similarly editorial despite shared backend.

### 5.4 Removal / persistence

- **[OBSERVED FACT]** GDM **generally does not remove profiles** at vendor request if product still marketed/sold and fits a category; instead will update to ensure accuracy.
- **[OBSERVED FACT]** May remove if vendor no longer in business and/or product no longer sold (clearly reflected on website) — either removes or marks "no longer sold/discontinued."
- **[OBSERVED FACT]** GDM reserves right to **modify, enhance or remove at any time** at sole discretion if profile fails Guidelines or "does not meet minimum standards of value and engagement." General rule, removal only if (i) no category fit or (ii) evidence product not actively marketed/sold.
- **[OBSERVED FACT]** During catalog maintenance, profiles discovered to be illegitimate/non-packaged B2B may be reviewed and removed. Contact: `listings@g2digitalmarkets.com`.

## 14. Strengths, Weaknesses & What To Exploit / Watch

### Strengths (as a platform)
- **[OBSERVED FACT + INFERENCE]** Scale + syndication: 1 profile → 3 domains → 2× SERP real estate vs single-directory competitors. 100M+ buyers and established SEO moat.
- **[OBSERVED FACT]** Human-advisor channel is defensible: no pure-self-serve directory replicates Software Advice's BANT call within 15 min. Higher-quality leads than clicks.
- **[OBSERVED FACT]** Friction-low free listing with permissive badge display encourages vendor proliferation (100K catalog vs GoodFirms 80K, Gartner Peer Insights ~5K).

### Weaknesses / Risks
- **[OBSERVED FACT]** Price opacity: true CPC after auction and PPL cost never published category-level; vendor must request estimate for traffic stats. **[INFERENCE]** Information asymmetry favours GDM — vendor can't benchmark without entering auction.
- **[OBSERVED FACT]** Methodologies shift without notice (Capterra Shortlist recency/weight changes 2025→2026; GetApp v6 vs v5 thresholds). **[INFERENCE]** Ranking volatility is feature, not bug — drives vendors to keep buying reviews/ads.
- **[OBSERVED FACT]** Taxonomy never unified across 3 sites under Gartner in 10 years — doubt G2 can unify 4 taxonomies quickly, especially with AI layer added. **[INFERENCE]** Vendor should treat convergence promises as 12–24-month horizon.
- **[OBSERVED FACT]** Post-merger concentration risk (Blastra, regulatory commentary): two dominant ecosystems → one owner → pricing power shifts to platform. TrustRadius/PeerSpot/SourceForge become only alternatives. No FTC block occurred; EU block also did not materialize despite speculation.

### Implications for an Enterprise Software Discovery Platform builder
- **[INFERENCE]** Clone the syndication pattern but invert the economics: single entry → multi-surface syndication is proven. Where GDM/G2 monetize incumbency via auction/subscription, a challenger wins by transparent per-category CPM + open methodology + AI summarization that is citation-faithful.
- **[INFERENCE]** RaaS-style review recruitment as a service is high-leverage and cheap vs paid search: vendor supplies contacts, platform funds $10–$25 gift, review syndicates → builds flywheel. Copy but disclose explicitly to avoid Trust & Safety risk.
- **[INFERENCE]** Software Advice's Calendly/phone advisor flow is replicable with AI voice + form qualification for long-tail categories — human advisors are expensive; hybrid qualification scales cheaper.
- **[INFERENCE]** Methodology pages are trust anchors — publish versioned methodology, corrections log, and moderation report (as G2 does quarterly; Software Advice does not) from day one.
