## How to duplicate in 30 seconds
1. Open link → click **Open in Figma** (needs free Figma account)
2. Click **Duplicate** (top right) → it copies to your Drafts
3. Change colors: select frame → Fill `#060A1A`, accent `#00E5FF` / `#7C4DFF` (see Design-System.md)
4. Export: File → Export → select assets
